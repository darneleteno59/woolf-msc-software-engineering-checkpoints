About Me

Behind the lens...

Hello! My name is Sara. My fascination with photography began the day I realized that an image could express what words sometimes fail to convey. Specializing in portraiture, lifestyle photography, and event photography, I strive above all to capture authenticity. No stiff poses or forced smiles. My approach is based on naturalness, spontaneous light, and building rapport. Whether it's for a professional project, a family session, or a unique event, I bring my expertise and sensitivity to your project to create images that truly reflect who you are.
